PAGE 08 / FAQ

Straight answers

Know what the next step means.

These answers reduce uncertainty without committing the business to unconfirmed response times, prices, warranties or authority outcomes.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

No round-the-clock availability is claimed in this template. Call to describe the issue and confirm current urgent availability. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Can you price the work from photos?

Photos may help route the request, but they may not show the cause, access conditions or full scope. The business should explain when an on-site assessment is needed before providing a written quote.

What should I send with a quote request?

Property type, city or postal/ZIP code, the affected area, what changed, urgency, preferred contact method and safe overview photos if useful.

How do I verify the licence?

Use the public authority linked on the Credentials page and compare the business name and licence details with the written quote. The template does not claim a number until the owner supplies a verifiable record.

Is an online request a confirmed booking?

No. A request starts a conversation. Assessment, scope acceptance, materials, access and any applicable authority steps can affect scheduling.

Will permits or inspections be needed?

That depends on the work and jurisdiction. The written scope should identify known permit or inspection responsibilities without promising an authority’s decision.

Do you provide a warranty?

No warranty is stated in this template. If the owner supplies written terms, publish the exact coverage, exclusions and duration without expanding them.

How are my details and photos used?

A production form should use the information only to review and respond to the enquiry, restrict access, define retention and explain any service providers in the Privacy notice. This preview does not send or store submissions.
