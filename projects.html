Publishing standard

Proof stays specific and permitted.

Never imply a project location, result, permit outcome or customer endorsement that cannot be documented.

Accurate location

Use the neighbourhood or city level agreed with the customer; avoid exposing a private address.

Accurate work

Describe what was actually completed without extending the caption into a broader warranty or compliance claim.

Image approval

Keep the owner’s source file and written permission record, and confirm any identifiable people may appear.
