Plain-language template

Privacy should be as clear as the scope.

This draft must be reviewed against the live form, vendors, retention practice and applicable law before launch.

Preview status: this static website does not transmit or store quote requests. Replace this notice when a production form is connected.

Information the live form may collect

Name, contact details, property type, general property location, requested service, urgency, preferred contact method, project description and optional photos. The form should not request identity documents, account numbers, payment data or other information that is not needed to respond.

Why the business uses it

To review the enquiry, contact the requester, understand the likely service path, plan an assessment and maintain an appropriate record of the customer conversation. A production operator should document any additional lawful purpose before collecting data for it.

Photos and location information

Photos should be used only to understand the service request unless separate written permission is obtained for another use. Customers should avoid including people, private documents, security information or precise details that are unnecessary. General location helps confirm coverage; precise access details should be handled carefully.

Service providers and access

Only people who need the information to respond or operate the approved form service should have access. Before launch, name or categorize the actual hosting, form, email, storage and security providers and explain whether information crosses borders.

Retention and deletion

Set a specific retention period based on real operational and legal needs. Delete unsuccessful enquiries and uploaded photos when no longer needed, subject to any documented legal obligations. Provide an owner-controlled process for access, correction and deletion requests where applicable.

Security

A production form should validate entries on the server, limit uploads, scan or isolate files, rate-limit abuse, encrypt traffic, restrict staff access and avoid placing customer details in public analytics, URLs or client-side code. No online method is risk-free.
